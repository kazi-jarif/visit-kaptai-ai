Kaptai Travel Rules

Important Visitor Rules

  1. 1. Always wear a life jacket when boating or kayaking on Kaptai Lake.
  2. 2. Photography is strictly prohibited near Kaptai Hydroelectric Dam & restricted defense zones.
  3. 3. Return from deep lake islands before 5:00 PM as evening fog and wind increase.
  4. 4. Keep Kaptai clean. Do not throw plastic bottles or wrappers into the lake.
  5. 5. Respect local hill community culture and temple dress codes at Chitmaram.

How to go

Take a Rangamati-bound bus from Chittagong city (Bahaddarhat or Oxygen area) and get off at Kaptai Rastar Matha (approx. 1.5 to 2 hours). From there, take a CNG or rickshaw to the lake ghat (10 to 15 minutes).

Estimated cost

Bus: ৳100–150 per person. CNG/rickshaw: ৳50–150. Total about ৳300–500 per person for a round trip.
